Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws. Stackbite is operated by Mert Demircan Yuce, California, USA. We collect consumer health data only to provide the features you use, and we never sell it.

  • Categories collected: foods and meals you log, calories and macros, calorie, macro and step goals, steps and active energy, height, weight, birth date or year, the sex used in the calorie formula, activity level and weight aim.
  • Why and how it is used: to show your daily totals and history, and to work out a daily calorie goal. Not for advertising.
  • Sources: you, when you enter it in the app; Apple Health for steps and active energy, only if you connect it.
  • Categories shared: your diary and details are stored by our hosting provider. Usage counts may include which built-in foods are added and searched for, never amounts, your weight or Apple Health data.
  • Third parties it is shared with: Supabase (hosting) and PostHog (usage counts), both acting only for us. No affiliates.
  • Your rights: see your data, delete it (Settings, then Account, then Delete account), withdraw consent by turning off usage counts, disconnecting Apple Health or deleting your account, and ask which third parties received it. Email stackbiteapp@gmail.com; we answer within 45 days. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to that email.
