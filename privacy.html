Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Stackbite does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. This policy explains what the Stackbite app ("the app") collects, why, and how you control it. The app is operated by Mert Demircan Yuce ("we", "us"), based in California, USA.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Food diary: foods and meals you log, portions, recipes and foods you create, calorie and macro goals, step goal and stepsWhen you use the appTo show your day, totals and history
Personal details: name, birth date or year, height, weight, the sex used in the calorie formula, activity level, weight aimWhen you enter them, for example in the first-launch introTo work out a daily calorie goal
Steps and active energy from Apple HealthOnly if you connect Apple HealthTo show your steps next to what you ate
Account: email address, an encrypted (hashed) password, display nameWhen you create an account, or sign in with Apple or GoogleTo sign you in and keep your diary on all your devices
Usage counts: which built-in foods are added, what is searched for, which screens open, steps of the intro, app version, device model, operating system and languageWhile you use the app, unless you turn it offTo learn what works and what to improve
Barcode numbersWhen you scan a productTo look up the product's label

Without an account, your diary stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Usage counts never include your email, the names of foods you create, how much you ate, or anything read from Apple Health. The camera is used only to read barcodes; no photos are taken or sent.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the app reads your steps and active energy, and nothing else. It never writes to Apple Health. The steps are saved with that day in your diary, and in your account if you have one, so they show on your other devices. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties for those purposes, and never sent to our usage counts. You can turn it off in the app's Settings, or remove access in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Stackbite.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (USA) hosts accounts and saved diaries.
  • PostHog (USA) receives usage counts. Session recording is off, and IP addresses are not stored.
  • Apple and Google sign you in if you choose those buttons. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • Open Food Facts receives a barcode number when you scan one, with no account details.
  • Apple App Store handles payments when subscriptions are offered; we never see your card.

These providers process data only to run the app for us. We share data with others only if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

Your diary and account stay until you delete them. Usage counts are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything you entered is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything saved to it at once; the diary on your phone stays until you remove the app.
  • Turn off usage counts: Settings, then Preferences, then Share usage counts.
  • Get a copy or ask a question: email mertdemircanyc@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

These rights apply wherever you live, including under California law. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Health data

Calorie, weight and diet details can count as health data under some state laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. We use it only to provide the app's features you ask for, never for advertising, and never sell it. Washington and Nevada residents can also read our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Children

Stackbite is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account can only read its own diary, enforced in the database itself. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if they are significant. Questions: mertdemircanyc@gmail.com.
